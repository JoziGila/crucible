# Decisions log

This log lists every decision of the twelve subsystem tabs in one line, with its reason and the latest round that added or revised it; each tab still owns its decisions, their full text and the alternatives it rejected. It holds 506 decisions: ING 29, ANV 40, FLX 56, COV 34, KLN 59, MEC 41, MFG 41, ELX 37, AGT 36, UIX 40, ECO 42 and PLT 51. Round 4, the third review's revision, added 65 and revised 306; the Status column names only the latest round, so it also shows 15 round 3 additions, 22 round 3 revisions, 42 round 2 changes, and 56 decisions as first drafted.

## Architecture decisions

The contract records one decision of its own and cites two tab decisions as binding on every tab. D-ECO-6 and D-ING-15 also appear in their tabs' tables above.

| Id | Decision | Contract section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-ARCH-1 | Three layers: versioned operations in native Rust inside Anvil and Flux, each released version freezing its source closure; sandboxed, determinized Wasm components for heavy custom algorithms, reaching operations only through `crucible:ops`; and features in Ingot for design intent, orchestration, entity selection, checks and UI metadata. | 4 |
| D-ECO-6 | The tier rule: Ingot unless code needs more than about 10⁶ primitive steps per call, an existing codebase in another language or a data structure Ingot cannot express efficiently, then a Wasm component; promotion to an operation when three independent packages need it, it needs GPU or SIMD kernels, or it must be differentiable through Flux's form-level derivatives, in public, with oracles. | 4 |
| D-ING-15 | The fallback: features may be authored in Rust as Wasm components through `#[ingot::feature]`, which generates the WIT world and a typed Ingot wrapper. | 4 |
