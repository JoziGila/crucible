# Decisions log

This log lists every decision of the twelve subsystem tabs in one line, with its reason and the latest round that added or revised it; each tab still owns its decisions, their full text and the alternatives it rejected. It holds 441 decisions: ING 27, ANV 36, FLX 46, COV 30, KLN 49, MEC 37, MFG 34, ELX 31, AGT 34, UIX 33, ECO 37 and PLT 47. Round 2 added 100 and revised 150, and round 3 added 61 and revised 162; because the Status column names only the latest round, it shows 44 round 2 additions and 72 round 2 revisions, and the other 102 decisions stand as first drafted.
