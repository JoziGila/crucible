# R8 geometry review

Summary: (1) Two identity guarantees fail as specified: definitional attributes (threads, holes, welds, finish, plies) sit outside `GeometryId`, the `ItemDefinition` and the `ApprovalSubject`, so a released part's thread can change with no new revision and no voided approval; and `Note::RankSwap` cannot be computed by a pure `anvil::resolve` Op, so anchored rank selectors and ordinal discriminators can still re-select silently (R8-01, R8-02).
(2) R2-03's fix does not hold for blends: the propagation table gives blend normals a bound from the spine's position alone, which is false without a spine tangent bound, and the error then flows through offsets into `Guaranteed` checks (R8-03).
(3) Several round 2 fixes contradict each other or cannot be built: `Interactive` topology differs from `SignOff` topology with no marker; the canonical example's check is `Undecided` by contract 7.1's own singular-point rule; the canonical edge role is undefined; copy naming depends on unrelated reads; statistical tolerance results depend on names; and the constraint solver's modular-rank "certificates" certify a rounded matrix, not the system (R8-04 to R8-09).

### R8-01 · Critical · Targets: CONTRACT, KLN, ANV, MEC
**Section:** Contract 5 (`ApprovalSubject`, `GeometryId`); KLN Revisions and lifecycle states (D-KLN-26, D-KLN-28); ANV Attributes and certificates, Encoding and versioning; MEC Holes and threads (D-MEC-7), Weldments
**Problem:** Design attributes that define the product but not its nominal geometry live in Anvil's name-keyed side table, which `GeometryId` excludes, and no tab says they enter "PMI". A cosmetic thread changed from M5 to M5×0.5, a weld throat from 4 mm to 3 mm, a surface finish, a ply's material or orientation, or a `HoleSpec` tolerance class leaves every `GeometryId` unchanged. D-KLN-26 then reports the released Item as unmodified, and an Approval of the old subject stays valid, although the part is now a different part. Contract 2 says "Any change to the subject voids it", but the subject omits the change.
**Evidence:** ANV: "Design attributes (`HoleSpec`, `ThreadSpec`, `Weld`, finish, the `FeatureRole` role aliases of contract 5) live in a side table keyed by `EntityId`"; "only `ObjectId` sees names, lineage and name-keyed attributes". KLN: "A revision's `ItemDefinition` holds the `GeometryId`s of the Part or Assembly with PMI attached by canonical entity number, the `Material` version, properties, the BOM by value and documents"; D-KLN-26: "an Item is modified since release exactly when its `ItemDefinition` differs". MEC D-MEC-7: threads default to `Repr::Cosmetic`, "nominal geometry plus the record"; weld beads are `Cosmetic` by default and "bead geometry is never needed". No tab defines PMI to include `ThreadSpec`, `HoleSpec`, `Weld`, finish or `Ply`.
**Fix:** In contract 5, add: "Every attribute class declares `definitional: bool`. Definitional attributes (GD&T and size tolerances, datums, `HoleSpec`, `ThreadSpec`, `Weld`, surface texture and finish, `Ply` and laminate records, region material assignments, late-bound field placeholders excluded) are encoded name-free, keyed by canonical entity number, in a definition section whose digest enters the `ItemDefinition` and the `ApprovalSubject` beside the `GeometryId`s." Replace "ItemDefinition GeometryIds with PMI attached by canonical entity number" with "ItemDefinition GeometryIds with definitional attributes attached by canonical entity number". `FeatureRole` aliases and display styles are non-definitional. Add KLN-O-PLM oracle: changing one attribute of each definitional class on a released golden-product Item marks it modified and voids its pending Approvals; changing a non-definitional one does neither.

### R8-02 · Critical · Targets: CONTRACT, ANV, ING, AGT
**Section:** Contract 5 `Query`; ANV Query resolution, EntityId (discriminators); ING The query language (rank selectors); AGT D-AGT-8; oracles ANV-O-NAME-2, AGT-O-18
**Problem:** The guarantee "Never silently picks another entity" rests on `Note::RankSwap`, emitted by `anvil::resolve` when "a winner whose lineage changes inside the declared parameter ranges". `anvil::resolve` is a pure Op over one Body and one `QueryPlan`; its key holds neither the design's parameter ranges nor any other evaluation, so it cannot know whether the winner changes elsewhere in the range. Even as a separate analysis, "full declared range of every parameter, endpoints included" has no sampling design (n parameters have 2ⁿ corners) and swaps between samples go unseen. A second silent path is the `Ordinal(k)` discriminator, ordered by canonical geometric order (box minimum first): any dimension edit that reorders two boxes swaps the ids, and only `Note::FragileAnchor` is raised.
**Evidence:** Contract 5: "success may carry Notes (`Merged`, `FragileAnchor`, `RankSwap`) that checks may forbid ... Never silently picks another entity." ANV: "a winner whose lineage changes inside the declared parameter ranges is reported as `Note::RankSwap { at }`"; "`Ordinal(k)`, which marks the id fragile (`Note::FragileAnchor`)"; canonical order is "kind, box minimum, box maximum, type and defining parameters". Contract 7.2: an `OpKey` holds the operation, arguments, input identities, class and fidelity, so a range-dependent note would be uncacheable.
**Fix:** (1) `anvil::resolve` reports only what it can compute locally: `Note::NearTie { runner_up, margin }` when the winner's certified margin in the ranked measure is below a declared relative band (default 5%). (2) Add an on-demand node `anvil::query_stability(query, ranges)` that evaluates a fixed, label-free design: each declared parameter at both endpoints one at a time, the current point ±10%, and 64 scrambled Sobol points over the declared box seeded from argument values; it reports `RankSwap { at }` with the sample, and its evidence is labelled `Sampled`, never a guarantee. Protected-branch CI runs it on every query that ends in a rank selector or reaches an `Ordinal` id. (3) Extent-use parameters fail with `ResolveError::Fragile { reason }` on a rank selector without `on_fragile: Accept`, and on an `Ordinal` id. (4) Replace the box-minimum ordinal with an order along the splitting tool's own role order where one exists. (5) Reword contract 5: "Never silently picks another entity at the evaluated configuration; stability across declared ranges is sampled evidence."

### R8-03 · Critical · Targets: ANV, CONTRACT
**Section:** ANV Conversions and error propagation (propagation table, D-ANV-21); contract 5 `Body`
**Problem:** R2-03's fix does not hold for blends. The rule "rolling-ball blend of radius r on a spine bounded by s: position s, normal 2s/r" uses only the spine's position bound, but a blend's normal is the direction from spine to surface in the plane normal to the spine's tangent, so it also turns with the spine's tangent error φ. The table gives intersection edges and spines no tangent bound ("— (edge)"), so φ is never computed. A spine within Hausdorff distance s can still tilt by much more than s/r. The "normal offset by d" rule also holds only where the approximant's offset is regular; a fitted face may carry curvature wiggles whose offset self-intersects where the exact offset does not.
**Evidence:** Reasoning: for supports with position bound p = 1 µm and normal bound n = 1 mrad meeting at θ_min = 60°, the spine's position bound is p/sin θ ≈ 1.15 µm, so the table gives the blend normal 2s/r ≈ 0.46 mrad for r = 5 mm. The spine's tangent is the normalized cross product of the two offset normals, so its direction error reaches about (n_a + n_b)/sin θ ≈ 2.3 mrad. A later 2 mm offset of that blend then gains a position error near 4.6 µm, while the table reports about 0.9 µm, so a `Guaranteed` `body.error() < 3µm` check passes on a reported 2.1 µm while the true bound exceeds 5 µm. Approximated faces occur in phase 1: D-ANV-7 flattens procedural nesting beyond depth three into certified caches "and records their bounds".
**Fix:** Extend `FaceBound` propagation with an edge tangent bound. Rows: intersection edge, spine or contact curve: position max(p_a, p_b)/sin θ_min, tangent (n_a + n_b)/sin θ_min; rolling-ball blend: position s, normal min(π, s/r + φ_spine + max(n_a, n_b)); normal offset by d: valid only when a certified bound κ̂ on the approximant's principal curvatures gives |d|·κ̂ ≤ 1/2, else `NotQuantified { OffsetNotRegular }`. Make ANV-O-CONV-2 perturb supports within both position and normal bounds, including tilted spines, and require the propagated normal bounds to hold. Add contract 5 wording: "propagation rules carry tangent and normal bounds, never position alone."

### R8-04 · Major · Targets: ANV, CONTRACT, ING, KLN
**Section:** ANV Fidelity (D-ANV-28), oracle ANV-O-FID-1; contract 7.1 Anvil; ING Fidelity, drags, holes and derivatives
**Problem:** At `Interactive` Anvil makes uncertified floating-point topology decisions, and ANV-O-FID-1 accepts that "differing `SignOff` decisions lie within ambiguity bands". A near-threshold decision that differs splits or merges faces, so `EntityId` discriminators, `ResolveError`s, query fixes accepted in the UI, drag-written seeds and agent Proposals built at `Interactive` can fail or resolve differently at `SignOff`. Nothing marks the `Interactive` result as resting on such a decision, and measurement bounds on it are reported as `Guaranteed`. Contract 7.1 allows only exact predicates and certified enclosures for decisions, with no fidelity exception.
**Evidence:** ANV: "`Interactive` | `Portable` floating-point topology decisions and sampled seeding, validated by `check_body`"; D-ANV-28 rejects "certified decisions at every fidelity (misses the rebuild targets)". Contract 7.1: "Exact adaptive predicates for linear decisions; certified enclosures with recorded, capped tolerance growth for decisions on curved geometry". ING: "A release demands every node at `SignOff`, geometry included".
**Fix:** Use filtered decisions at `Interactive`, as Shewchuk's adaptive predicates do: compute the distance in `f64` with a forward error bound e, decide directly when |q − τ| > e, and otherwise run the certified three-way rule. Topology then agrees across fidelities except where `SignOff` itself fails with `Ambiguous`. Keep sampled seeding and uncertified tracing at `Interactive`, since `check_body`'s global separation catches missed branches and forces recomputation. Change ANV-O-FID-1 to "`Interactive` and `SignOff` results have equal topology and equal `EntityId`s on the corpus". If the filter is rejected, the contract must state the exception and ING must make every query resolved on an `Interactive` Body `Pending` when a provisional decision touched its anchors.

### R8-05 · Major · Targets: CONTRACT, FLX, ING
**Section:** Contract 6 canonical example; contract 7.1 Flux (singular points); FLX Regularized goals and singular points; ING Checks (outcome table)
**Problem:** R1-05's fix excludes only the clamp, but R1-05 also named "edges of loaded or fixed patches", and contract 7.1 keeps them in the singular list. The canonical check `stress.max(over: p.away_from(clamp, 2 * thickness))` includes the edges of the loaded face `web.face(role: end_cap)`, so by contract 7.1 and FLX's rule the check is always `Undecided { SingularPoint }`. ING's outcome table shows it passing. The first example every author copies still teaches a check that cannot decide.
**Evidence:** Contract 7.1: "Singular points (re-entrant edges, edges where the boundary-condition type or material changes, contact boundaries, edges of fixed or loaded patches) make point quantities `Undecided { SingularPoint }` unless the check names a region excluding a stated distance from them." FLX: "Flux flags singular sets from the Body and the study's conditions: ... edges of fixed or loaded patches". Contract 6: `static_stress(p, fixed: clamp, force: load, on: web.face(role: end_cap))`.
**Fix:** Make the flag geometric rather than blanket: a loaded patch's edge is singular only where the traction has a tangential component at the edge or the patch boundary lies inside a face; a uniform normal traction on a whole face meeting free faces at convex edges is not flagged. FLX states this test in its singular-set capability with an oracle on a 90° corner under normal and shear tractions. Independently, rewrite the canonical line as `study.von_mises.max(over: p.away_from([clamp, web.face(role: end_cap)], 2 * thickness))`, so the example holds under either rule, and update ING, AGT and the overview copies.

### R8-06 · Major · Targets: CONTRACT, ANV, MEC, ING
**Section:** Contract 6 canonical example; ANV EntityId (role vocabularies); MEC Roles, queries and fixes; ING The query language (Role)
**Problem:** No tab defines the roles `extrude` gives edges, yet the canonical example and MEC filter edges by `side(e)`, which ANV lists only as a face role. The canonical `base.face(role: end_cap).edge(role: side(outline.top))` needs the cap edge next to the face swept from `outline.top`; MEC's `block.edges(role: side(any), parallel_to: z)` needs the edges where side faces meet, which come from profile vertices. ING defines a role filter as "entities created with that role", under which neither query has a meaning, since no edge carries a `side` role. Reading `role:` on edges as adjacency to faces of that role would make both work, but no tab says so, and it contradicts ING's definition.
**Evidence:** ANV: "the role comes from the operation's vocabulary (`start_cap`, `end_cap`, `side(e)` for `extrude`)", listing face roles only. ING query table: "Role | ... | Entities created with that role". Contract 6 and MEC D-MEC-6 example `fillet(block.edges(role: side(any), parallel_to: z), 5mm)`.
**Fix:** Define edge and vertex roles in ANV's role vocabulary for every sweeping operation: `extrude` creates edges `start(e)` and `end(e)` per profile edge, `lateral(v)` per profile vertex, and vertices `start(v)` and `end(v)`; `revolve` and `sweep` follow the same pattern. Rewrite the canonical line as `base.edge(role: end(outline.top))` and MEC's as `block.edges(role: lateral(any))`. Add ING-O and MEC-O-PF-1 cases that compile and resolve every role of every std sweeping feature, and a language-service rule that a role filter on the wrong entity kind is a compile error.

### R8-07 · Major · Targets: ANV, ING, MEC
**Section:** ANV Copies and placements (D-ANV-30); ING Call sites and names; MEC Patterns, mirror and hulls
**Problem:** Whether a transform keeps names depends on whether its source binding is used again anywhere in the program. Adding a read-only use of the source, such as a volume check, a drawing view or a measurement, turns `x |> move(...)` from a placement into a copy, renames every downstream entity with role `copy`, and cuts lineage, so queries anchored on the source's roles fail far from the edit. Naming then depends on code that never touches the geometry, which contradicts the `OpSite` rule that statement identity is "stable under edits elsewhere".
**Evidence:** ANV: "A transform that replaces its source in the data flow keeps `EntityId`s. One whose source is used again, or written as `copy`, creates `EID(kind, site, copy, [source])` ... followed by lineage only on request (`.copies()`)". ING: "The compiler knows reuse from the IIR's data flow, and a transform that replaces its source keeps names."
**Fix:** Decide copying where names would collide, not by global reuse. Transforms always keep names. An operation that receives two operands carrying the same `EntityId` (a Boolean, an n-ary `combine`, an assembly-level feature) relabels the operand that came from the later transform in program order with role `copy` or `mirror`, keyed by that transform's `OpSite`, and records `Created { role: copy, from: [source] }`. An explicit `copy(x)` keeps today's meaning. ANV-O-COPY-1 adds a case where a read-only use of the source is added and no `EntityId` of the transformed body changes.

### R8-08 · Major · Targets: ING, MEC, CONTRACT
**Section:** ING Arithmetic on tolerances (symbol keys), Determinism and randomness; MEC Stack-up and variation analysis (D-MEC-22); contract 7.1 Label invariance
**Problem:** Contract 7.1 requires statistics to be invariant under renaming `EntityId`s and `OpSite`s, but tolerance noise symbols are identified by site, by `EntityId` and by Instance path, and nothing says how symbols are mapped to random streams or Sobol dimensions. If the mapping follows symbol identity or order of appearance by name, a binding rename reassigns Sobol dimensions and changes `.monte_carlo` and `variation` results bit for bit. D-MEC-22 orders contributors by linear sensitivity, but ties are common (four identical holes, symmetric stacks), and code-level sites have no geometry to break them.
**Evidence:** ING: "A code-level site has one symbol"; "A tolerance forwarded into a Part is keyed by (site, realizing Part or Instance path)"; "A tolerance read from geometry ... is keyed by the aspect's `EntityId` and Instance path". Contract 7.1: "geometry, connectivity, Field values and statistics are invariant under any injective renaming". ANV-O-LABEL-1 compares check values after re-randomizing names.
**Fix:** Specify the assignment in ING: symbols are ordered by (|first-order sensitivity of the measured quantity|, descending; then the name-free canonical hash of the symbol's position in the expression DAG, with nominal, limits and distribution; then the canonical entity number of the realizing aspect). Symbols equal under every key are exchangeable: the measured function is symmetric in them, so any assignment gives identical bits. Streams and Sobol dimensions follow this order; seeds hash argument values and `GeometryId`s only. Add ANV-O-LABEL-1 cases with `.monte_carlo`, `variation` and `yield` checks.

### R8-09 · Major · Targets: ANV, MEC, ING
**Section:** ANV Constraint solving; MEC Solver method (D-MEC-4), Diagnosis; ING Builder blocks and sketches; oracles MEC-O-SK-2, ANV-O-SOLVE-1
**Problem:** Modular rank certifies the rank of a rational matrix exactly, but the Jacobians here are rounded. At a solved configuration, rounding perturbs a structurally rank-deficient Jacobian into a generically full-rank one, so "full rank certified" is reported for a sketch that is over-constrained (three angles summing to 180°, a rotated rectangle with four right angles). The witness configuration method requires a witness that satisfies the incidence and tangency constraints exactly; a witness built from "metric values that are rationals hashed from arguments" must still be solved for, and its coordinates are irrational whenever circles and tangencies are involved. Separately, under-determined blocks resolved by minimum displacement have no stated certification, so the commonest sketches (under-constrained ones) yield only `Estimated` points.
**Evidence:** MEC Diagnosis: "Rank at a witness configuration whose metric values are rationals hashed from arguments and seed geometry"; "Rank modulo one or two 61-bit primes certifies full rank". Zou and Feng (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.00526, opened): the Jacobian must be evaluated at a witness "which has satisfied all the constraints with degenerate in the GCS, for example, parallel and incident constraints", and the method's well- and under-constraint criteria "may give wrong results in some situations". MEC-O-SK-2 tests "known dependencies" against "exact rational rank", which passes only when test witnesses are exactly rational.
**Fix:** (1) Build witnesses constructively in the decomposition's order with rational choices (pick tangent points and directions, then centres along normals), so incidences hold exactly; use modular rank only on such exact witnesses. (2) Where no rational witness exists, compute rank at a Krawczyk-certified witness with interval arithmetic: a nonzero interval minor certifies full rank, and a deficiency is reported `Estimated` with the singular-value gap. (3) Certify under-determined blocks by applying Krawczyk to the square KKT system of the minimum-displacement problem, which is regular at a strict minimizer, so their points can be `Guaranteed`. (4) MEC-O-SK-2 adds witnesses with circles and tangencies whose coordinates are irrational.

### R8-10 · Major · Targets: ANV, MEC
**Section:** ANV Measurement services, operation contract (`thickness` grade); MEC Molded and cast part design (D-MEC-18); ANV Open questions 5
**Problem:** `thickness` is graded `Certified` because "the GPU locates candidate thin regions, and the CPU computes every canonical value". The minimum thickness and its location are global properties: if the GPU's `f32` sampling misses a region, the CPU never evaluates it, and the result depends on whether and which GPU ran. ANV open question 5 says WGSL's accuracy is unverified, so GPU candidate sets cannot be shown conservative. Ties in the minimum (symmetric walls) also need a canonical location rule, or the reported location follows search order.
**Evidence:** ANV: "Thickness is `Certified`: the GPU locates candidate thin regions, and the CPU computes every canonical value"; "WGSL offers `f32`, `f16` and AbstractFloat, with accuracy not yet verified for intervals [unverified], so GPU results only order, cull and preview". Contract 7.3: `Certified` means "output independent of the class that computed intermediates".
**Fix:** Answer ANV open question 5 with a rule: GPU floating-point results never prune; they only order work and seed upper bounds. The CPU proves the minimum by branch-and-bound over the whole Body with certified lower bounds, so the GPU changes speed, never the result. Report the location as the canonically first point (canonical face number, then (u, v) on a fixed dyadic grid) among those whose certified value lies within the decision band of the minimum. Add an oracle that disables the GPU, and one that feeds it adversarial candidates, and requires identical bits.

### R8-11 · Major · Targets: CONTRACT, ANV, ING, MEC
**Section:** Contract 5 `Query`; ANV Query resolution (outcome table, D-ANV-15); ING The query language; MEC Specification model (GD&T targets)
**Problem:** The extent rule covers merges but not deletions. When some descendants of an anchor are deleted before the consuming operation, the query "resolves to the survivors with `Note::Partial`", so a fillet on a face's edges silently runs on fewer edges and an all-over profile or datum target silently covers fewer faces. D-ANV-15's own reason for failing merged extents, that the extent a fillet or GD&T target means has changed, applies equally to a partial deletion.
**Evidence:** ANV outcome table: "Some descendants deleted later | Resolves to the survivors with `Note::Partial { deleted }`". D-ANV-15: "a merged face keeps the surface a sketch plane needs but changes the extent a fillet or GD&T target means". Contract 5 names only the merge case for extent use.
**Fix:** In contract 5 and ANV, for extent-use parameters a partial deletion fails with `ResolveError::Partial { anchor, deleted, fixes }` unless `on_partial: Accept` is written; surface use keeps the note. Fixes offer "accept the survivors" and "anchor on the surviving pieces". ANV-O-NAME-2 adds a case where a later pocket removes one face of a profile target and one edge of a fillet chain.

### R8-12 · Major · Targets: ANV, MEC, ING, CONTRACT
**Section:** Contract 5 `Tol<T>`; ING `Tol<T>` (D-ING-4); ANV Attributes and certificates; MEC Specification model (D-MEC-27)
**Problem:** A `Tol` forwarded into a feature becomes PMI on the entities that feature creates, with no explicit control statement in source. When a later operation splits such a face, ANV's `Fail` split policy removes the attribute from the pieces and emits only `Note::AttributeSplit`, while MEC says the control "fails with `ToleranceTargetSplit`". For a forwarded tolerance there is no control to fail or re-target, so if ANV's worked example (a rib across the plate's top face) wrote `extrude(4mm ± 0.1mm)`, the thickness tolerance would disappear from the drawing and the AP242 file unless someone read a Note. The two tabs also disagree on whether a split is a Note or a failure.
**Evidence:** ANV: "a `Fail` split, which GD&T and PMI attributes use (D-MEC-27), leaves the pieces without the attribute and reports `Note::AttributeSplit { class, pieces }`". MEC D-MEC-27: "When a later operation splits a toleranced face, the control fails with `ToleranceTargetSplit { control, pieces }`". Contract 5: "the tolerance's site is forwarded to the feature that realizes it, which attaches it as PMI".
**Fix:** (1) A realizing feature records forwarded PMI as an anchored aspect query (`OfSize { faces: [start_cap, end_cap] }` keyed by its `OpSite`), re-resolved on the final Part like an explicit control, instead of an attribute copied through every operation. (2) Every PMI item, forwarded or explicit, has an `Error`-severity check `pmi_target_resolved` that fails with `ToleranceTargetSplit` or `ResolveError` and never degrades to a Note. (3) The fix for a forwarded item inserts an explicit `part.tolerance(...)` statement. ANV keeps the Note for the operation and MEC owns the failure; both tabs say so in one sentence. Add an oracle on the worked example with `4mm ± 0.1mm` forwarded.

### R8-13 · Major · Targets: KLN, ANV
**Section:** KLN Proposals and merge (condition 5, D-KLN-21); ANV HistoryRecord and lineage
**Problem:** Merge condition 5 flags a conflict when "both sides modify, split or delete the same base entity", but no tab defines what a side "modifies" relative to the base. If it means "same `EntityId`, different geometry", then a dimension change on one side (which moves every adjacent face) and a new hole on the other (which adds a loop to one of those faces) conflict, which is exactly the pair D-KLN-21 cites for rejecting region overlap. If it means `HistoryRecord`s, a parameter edit re-runs the operation and produces no `Modified` record against the base at all.
**Evidence:** KLN condition 5: "Both sides modify, split or delete the same base entity, or one side's new entity intersects the other side's modified region at or below the merge clearance". D-KLN-21: "*Rejected:* ... overlap of changed regions (flags the commonest pair of edits: a dimension change beside a new feature)". ANV `Modified { id, aspects }` already distinguishes geometry, boundary, tolerance, attributes and `Mirrored`.
**Fix:** Define each side's touched set as the base entities that carry a topological record (`Split`, `Merged`, `Deleted`, or `Modified` with aspect `Boundary` or `Attributes`) in an Op whose key differs between base and that side; geometry-only changes from parameter edits are excluded. A conflict is a nonempty intersection of the two touched sets, or a created entity on one side within the merge clearance (certified distance) of an entity in the other side's touched set. Add KLN oracles: width change plus a new hole on the same face merges clean; two fillets on one edge conflict; a hole and a pocket that both cut one face conflict only when they touch.

### R8-14 · Major · Targets: ANV
**Section:** ANV Resolution, frames and scale (D-ANV-3); Open questions 3
**Problem:** Resolution is history-dependent and discontinuous. `ρ_out = max(input ρ, ρ(D_out))` takes the coarsest input, tool bodies included, so a `ThroughAll` cut, a split by a large sheet or a mould block coarsens the part permanently: identical final geometry built two ways gets two resolutions, two `GeometryId`s, two `τ_cap`s and different later decisions. Separately, ρ doubles when the diagonal crosses a power of two, so a parameter sweep or an optimizer over the canonical bracket's 20 to 400 mm `width` crosses three jumps in λ and τ_cap (at diagonals of 62.5, 125 and 250 mm).
**Evidence:** ANV: "A result takes ρ_out = max(input ρ, ρ(D_out))"; ρ(D) = 2^(⌈log₂ D⌉ − 30); minimum size λ = 2⁴ρ and growth cap τ_cap = 2¹⁰ρ. D-ANV-3 rejects "ρ from the exact diagonal (changes with every edit)" but power-of-two rounding still changes it at every crossing.
**Fix:** Set ρ_out = ρ(D_out) of the result alone. Inputs' coarser tolerances survive where they matter, as per-entity τ on the entities that carry them (already stored when τ ≠ ρ), so tools never coarsen the result. State the jump explicitly and let a design pin `resolution:` per Part for sweeps and optimization, with `Note::ResolutionStep` when a pinned value is more than a factor 2 from ρ(D). Add an oracle: the same final geometry built with tool bodies of different sizes has one `GeometryId`. Answer open question 3: freeze ρ at 2⁻³⁰ now, since it governs determinism headroom, and let the phase 0 ABC measurement choose τ_cap's exponent (2¹⁰ today) and τ_heal's default instead, which are the quantities import growth actually stresses.

### R8-15 · Major · Targets: ANV, MEC, CONTRACT
**Section:** ANV Surface–surface intersection (phase 1 scope), Blends (stage table), capability tables for `sweep` and `offset`; MEC Holes and threads (D-MEC-7), Phasing; contract 13.2 Phase 1 scope
**Problem:** Phase 1 certifies SSI only among planes, quadrics, tori, extrusion and revolution surfaces, NURBS–analytic pairs and the NURBS pairs GP1 and the prismatic ABC corpus need. Stage 1 blends, also phase 1, promise "procedural blend, offset and swept supports", whose spines need intersections of offsets of rolling-ball and swept faces, and MEC's phase 1 printed threads (`Repr::Full`, "an exact helical sweep and Boolean") need helicoidal sweep faces intersected with cylinders, cones and planes. Neither pair class is in the phase 1 SSI list, so these phase 1 capabilities either fail or quietly depend on phase 2 work that contract 13.2 defers.
**Evidence:** ANV: "Phase 1 certifies SSI among planes, quadrics, tori, extrusion and revolution surfaces, NURBS–analytic pairs and the NURBS pairs that GP1 and the prismatic ABC corpus need; general NURBS–NURBS pairs follow in phase 2". Stage 1 row: "procedural blend, offset and swept supports". `anvil::sweep` phase 1 with "helical sweeps for threads"; MEC Phasing 1: "GP2: ... printed threads".
**Fix:** Name the phase 1 procedural pair classes explicitly and give them oracles: canal surfaces (rolling-ball blends on analytic supports) and their offsets against planes, quadrics, tori and other canal surfaces; helicoids against planes, cylinders and cones. Restrict the stage-1 subset of the re-fillet benchmark to supports in the phase 1 SSI classes. For GP2's printed threads, build the full thread on the mesh path (tessellate the sweep within the 3MF chord tolerance and use the mesh Boolean), which 3MF output needs anyway, and keep exact B-rep threads in phase 2.

### R8-16 · Major · Targets: ANV, MEC

**Problem:** ANV says feature-adaptive subdivision "computes the true limit up to machine precision" near extraordinary vertices and creases, and in the next paragraph that a fixed adaptive depth there "stops short of the limit" with a recorded bound. Contract 5 still lists SubD ⊂ BRep among inclusions "without approximating geometry".
**Evidence:** ANV: "feature-adaptive subdivision, which computes the true limit up to machine precision [Nießner et al. 2012]"; "Where a fixed adaptive depth near creases and extraordinary vertices stops short of the limit, ... a certified bound per depth".
**Fix:** State one policy: subdivide until the remaining irregular region's certified bound falls below ρ/2⁸, else stop at a depth cap and record the bound. Amend contract 5: "SubD ⊂ BRep is exact except near extraordinary vertices with semi-sharp creases, where a recorded bound applies."

### R8-42 · Minor · Targets: ANV, MEC
**Section:** ANV Construction (`anvil::wrap`); MEC Deform, wrap, emboss and engrave
**Problem:** Emboss "through `offset_faces`" is ill-defined: offsetting an imprinted region of a cylinder moves it along the normal, and its neighbour is the rest of the same cylinder, so "neighbours extended" creates no side walls.
**Evidence:** ANV: "`anvil::wrap` | ... emboss and engrave through `offset_faces`"; `offset_faces`: "Faces moved along normals; neighbours extended".
**Fix:** Specify emboss and engrave as sweeping the imprinted region along the face normal by the height (side walls are normal-ruled procedural surfaces) followed by a union or difference; the walls meet the base face along the wrapped curves known by construction, so no new SSI class is needed. Add the case to ANV-O-PROJ-2.

### R8-43 · Minor · Targets: MEC
**Section:** MEC Interference and clearance (`IntendedFit`)
**Problem:** Geometry is built at nominal, so a shaft and bore with an interference fit (10 H7/p6) are modelled at the same 10 mm and classify as `Touching`, never as "Overlap within a declared ISO 286 interference fit". The class exists but cannot occur, and press fits go unreported to tolerancing.
**Evidence:** MEC table: "`IntendedFit` | Overlap within a declared ISO 286 interference fit | Ignored, reported to tolerancing". ING D-ING-4: geometry stays at nominal.
**Fix:** Classify a `Touching` cylindrical pair as `IntendedFit` when both aspects carry fit codes whose limits interfere, and report the worst-case and statistical interference from the `Tol` limits, not from geometry. MEC-O-AS-3 adds a press fit at nominal.

### R8-44 · Minor · Targets: MEC, MFG

### R8-48 · Minor · Targets: ANV, PLT, KLN
**Section:** ANV Open questions 1 (D-ANV-18)
**Problem:** The CI cost of keeping every version executable is open.
**Evidence:** ANV open question 1: "PLT and Kiln must confirm the CI cost."
**Fix:** Decide: per-merge CI reproduces golden hashes only for `Current` and `Deprecated` versions pinned by a golden product or a registry package; `Retired` versions run from archived Wasm builds and are re-verified on every toolchain, `detmath` or Wasmtime upgrade and weekly in shards. ### R8-49 · Minor · Targets: ANV, CONTRACT
**Section:** ANV Open questions 2, GPU use and freedom to operate (D-ANV-17)
**Problem:** The freedom-to-operate opinion has no deadline and no fallback, although SSI is chain A's first research item.
**Evidence:** ANV open question 2: "Counsel's opinion ... is pending"; contract 7.1: "until the opinion arrives, SSI itself is a phase 1 release risk".
**Fix:** Decide: the opinion is due by month 9 as a phase 0 gate item. Until it is favourable for the GPU path, phase 1 ships SSI without GPU sampling, which is only a speed path (D-ANV-17's ≥ 3× target), and keeps the certified-root design that answers claim 16. Reason: removing the sampling path removes the part closest to the claimed point-set comparisons at a known cost.

### R8-50 · Minor · Targets: ANV
**Section:** ANV Open questions 6; Conversions (Implicit → BRep, fitted)
**Problem:** Fitted implicit-to-B-rep faces have no certification method.
**Evidence:** ANV open question 6: "Fitted implicit-to-B-rep faces have no verified guarantee".
**Fix:** Decide: a fitted face S is `Guaranteed` when interval evaluation of f∘S over (u, v) boxes bounds |f| and a certified lower bound on |∇f| in the band gives the one-sided distance |f|/|∇f|_min, and a normal-cone test on the band proves the closest-point map from the zero set to S is one-to-one, giving the other side; the normal bound comes from the angle between ∇f and S's normal over the same boxes. Reason: it reuses the certification already used for implicit meshing.

### R8-51 · Minor · Targets: ING
**Section:** ING Open questions 4
**Problem:** Affine arithmetic at scale is open.
**Evidence:** ING open question 4: "Stacks over 10⁵ sites may need symbol condensation".
**Fix:** Decide: forms store coefficients sparsely, since most stacks touch few symbols; when a form exceeds 1,024 symbols, keep the 1,024 largest |coefficients| and fold the rest into the remainder, reporting `Note::SymbolsCondensed { count, width }`. Reason: folding into the remainder keeps `.limits()` a guaranteed enclosure and loses correlation only for the folded symbols, and the note keeps the approximation explicit.
