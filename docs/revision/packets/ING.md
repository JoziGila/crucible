### R7-01 · Critical · Targets: CONTRACT, FLX, COV, MEC, ING
**Section:** Contract 5 (`ErrorBound`, `ErrorBudget`), 7.1 Flux; FLX "Calibrated estimates" (D-FLX-24); COV "Error budgets and model form" (D-COV-17); MEC "Design checks" (D-MEC-30)
**Problem:** Every phase 1 `SignOff` stress and buckling check passes on a calibrated `Estimated` bound, and that bound carries only a marginal 99% guarantee over FLX's corpus. This is the failure mode R1-04 found for surrogates, and the contract fixed it only for surrogates: topology optimization and agents that minimize mass subject to a passing check select exactly the designs where the estimator undershoots, so coverage on optimized designs falls below the corpus rate. The calibration miss rate also never enters the budget's confidence: contract 5 union-bounds only "statistical, conformal, input distributions", so a `Pass` on an η99 bound reports no confidence at all. Finally, a class is a label the model author assigns (D-COV-17), with no computable test that a given result belongs to the population the 300 samples came from.
**Evidence:** FLX D-FLX-24: η99 is "the 99th-percentile underestimate of that estimator on its problem class". FLX-O-ERR-3: "Bound at least the true error in at least 99% of held-out cases per class". MEC D-MEC-30: "physics checks accept, at `SignOff`, calibrated `Estimated` bounds". Contract 7.1 applies the selection argument only to surrogates: "inside optimization loops calibration uses weighted conformal prediction". MEC's class for GP1 and GP2, "Linear elasticity, regularized peak, immersed octree", spans every geometry, cut fraction, Poisson's ratio and distance to a singular set.
**Fix:** (1) Contract 5: `Estimated { calibration }` carries its exceedance level α (0.01 for η99) and joins the union bound with the other confidence-carrying parts, so every `CheckOutcome` reports its total confidence. (2) Each calibration class in `std::sim::calibration` carries an applicability envelope over computable estimator features: degree p, minimum cut-cell volume fraction, cells per smoothing radius (ρ/h), distance to the nearest flagged singular set in units of ρ, Poisson's ratio and material contrast. A result outside its class envelope reports `Indicator`. (3) A `SignOff` `Pass` resting on an `Estimated` discretization part needs a refinement-consistency confirmation: one further level (h/2 or p + 1) whose value lies inside the first bound and whose own bound is smaller. Kiln's cost model counts the extra solve. (4) Every class corpus includes at least 20% adversarial samples, produced by `std::opt` runs that minimize the check margin inside the class envelope, and FLX-O-ERR-3 adds an adversarial search for undershoots, as FLX-O-ROM-2 does for surrogates. (5) Contract 7.1's sentence "Interactive bounds are reported already multiplied by their calibrated factor" becomes "every `Estimated` bound", matching D-FLX-24.

### R7-02 · Critical · Targets: CONTRACT, FLX, ING
**Section:** Contract 6 canonical example and 7.1 singular points; FLX "Regularized goals and singular points"; ING "Checks" outcome table
**Problem:** Three different singular-point rules exist, and under two of them the canonical check can never pass. The contract makes a point quantity `Undecided` "unless the check names a region excluding a stated distance from" every flagged set. ING makes it `Undecided` when "the region comes within the exclusion distance". FLX makes it `Undecided` when the quantity is within the exclusion distance. FLX's detector flags "edges of fixed or loaded patches", and the canonical check's region `p.away_from(clamp, 2 * thickness)` excludes the clamp but not the loaded `web.face(role: end_cap)`, so under the contract and ING the canonical example is always `Undecided { SingularPoint }`. ING's own `bracket_check` (`b.away_from(clamp, 8mm)` with a loaded `pad`) fails the same way. The detector is also blind to traction compatibility: a uniform normal traction over a whole face bounded by convex 90° edges is compatible at the corner and not singular, while a shear traction meeting a free face is.
**Evidence:** Contract 7.1: "edges of fixed or loaded patches) make point quantities `Undecided { SingularPoint }` unless the check names a region excluding a stated distance from them." Contract 6: `static_stress(p, fixed: clamp, force: load, on: web.face(role: end_cap))` and `max(over: p.away_from(clamp, 2 * thickness))`. ING table: "The region comes within the exclusion distance of a flagged singular edge | `Undecided { SingularPoint }`". At a 90° corner with traction t on face x and a free face y, equilibrium needs σ_xy = t_y on one face and σ_xy = 0 on the other, so only a shear component is incompatible.
**Fix:** State one rule in contract 7.1 and point to it from FLX and ING: (1) The detector flags a load or support patch boundary only where the prescribed condition is incompatible there: a patch boundary inside a smooth face, a corner where a shear traction meets a free face, or a clamped face meeting a free face. Re-entrant edges and material junctions stay flagged. (2) Every flagged set carries a default exclusion distance, max(2ρ, local wall thickness), which a check may override. (3) A regularized maximum is `Undecided { SingularPoint }` only when its near-maximal set (locations whose regularized value lies within the bound of the maximum) meets the exclusion zone of a flagged set; a region that merely contains a flagged set is not `Undecided`. (4) Rewrite ING's row accordingly. (5) Make the canonical example explicit about both ends: `max(over: p.away_from([clamp, web.face(role: end_cap)], 2 * thickness))`, and add an oracle that the canonical bracket decides at `SignOff`.

### R8-02 · Critical · Targets: CONTRACT, ANV, ING, AGT
**Section:** Contract 5 `Query`; ANV Query resolution, EntityId (discriminators); ING The query language (rank selectors); AGT D-AGT-8; oracles ANV-O-NAME-2, AGT-O-18
**Problem:** The guarantee "Never silently picks another entity" rests on `Note::RankSwap`, emitted by `anvil::resolve` when "a winner whose lineage changes inside the declared parameter ranges". `anvil::resolve` is a pure Op over one Body and one `QueryPlan`; its key holds neither the design's parameter ranges nor any other evaluation, so it cannot know whether the winner changes elsewhere in the range. Even as a separate analysis, "full declared range of every parameter, endpoints included" has no sampling design (n parameters have 2ⁿ corners) and swaps between samples go unseen. A second silent path is the `Ordinal(k)` discriminator, ordered by canonical geometric order (box minimum first): any dimension edit that reorders two boxes swaps the ids, and only `Note::FragileAnchor` is raised.
**Evidence:** Contract 5: "success may carry Notes (`Merged`, `FragileAnchor`, `RankSwap`) that checks may forbid ... Never silently picks another entity." ANV: "a winner whose lineage changes inside the declared parameter ranges is reported as `Note::RankSwap { at }`"; "`Ordinal(k)`, which marks the id fragile (`Note::FragileAnchor`)"; canonical order is "kind, box minimum, box maximum, type and defining parameters". **Fix:** (1) `anvil::resolve` reports only what it can compute locally: `Note::NearTie { runner_up, margin }` when the winner's certified margin in the ranked measure is below a declared relative band (default 5%). Protected-branch CI runs it on every query that ends in a rank selector or reaches an `Ordinal` id. (3) Extent-use parameters fail with `ResolveError::Fragile { reason }` on a rank selector without `on_fragile: Accept`, and on an `Ordinal` id. (4) Replace the box-minimum ordinal with an order along the splitting tool's own role order where one exists. (5) Reword contract 5: "Never silently picks another entity at the evaluated configuration; stability across declared ranges is sampled evidence."

### R8-07 · Major · Targets: ANV, ING, MEC
**Section:** ANV Copies and placements (D-ANV-30); ING Call sites and names; MEC Patterns, mirror and hulls
**Problem:** Whether a transform keeps names depends on whether its source binding is used again anywhere in the program. Adding a read-only use of the source, such as a volume check, a drawing view or a measurement, turns `x |> move(...)` from a placement into a copy, renames every downstream entity with role `copy`, and cuts lineage, so queries anchored on the source's roles fail far from the edit. Naming then depends on code that never touches the geometry, which contradicts the `OpSite` rule that statement identity is "stable under edits elsewhere".
**Evidence:** ANV: "A transform that replaces its source in the data flow keeps `EntityId`s. One whose source is used again, or written as `copy`, creates `EID(kind, site, copy, [source])` ... followed by lineage only on request (`.copies()`)". ING: "The compiler knows reuse from the IIR's data flow, and a transform that replaces its source keeps names."
**Fix:** Decide copying where names would collide, not by global reuse. Transforms always keep names. An operation that receives two operands carrying the same `EntityId` (a Boolean, an n-ary `combine`, an assembly-level feature) relabels the operand that came from the later transform in program order with role `copy` or `mirror`, keyed by that transform's `OpSite`, and records `Created { role: copy, from: [source] }`. An explicit `copy(x)` keeps today's meaning. ANV-O-COPY-1 adds a case where a read-only use of the source is added and no `EntityId` of the transformed body changes.

### R8-09 · Major · Targets: ANV, MEC, ING
**Section:** ANV Constraint solving; MEC Solver method (D-MEC-4), Diagnosis; ING Builder blocks and sketches; oracles MEC-O-SK-2, ANV-O-SOLVE-1
**Problem:** Modular rank certifies the rank of a rational matrix exactly, but the Jacobians here are rounded. At a solved configuration, rounding perturbs a structurally rank-deficient Jacobian into a generically full-rank one, so "full rank certified" is reported for a sketch that is over-constrained (three angles summing to 180°, a rotated rectangle with four right angles). The witness configuration method requires a witness that satisfies the incidence and tangency constraints exactly; a witness built from "metric values that are rationals hashed from arguments" must still be solved for, and its coordinates are irrational whenever circles and tangencies are involved. Separately, under-determined blocks resolved by minimum displacement have no stated certification, so the commonest sketches (under-constrained ones) yield only `Estimated` points.
**Evidence:** MEC Diagnosis: "Rank at a witness configuration whose metric values are rationals hashed from arguments and seed geometry"; "Rank modulo one or two 61-bit primes certifies full rank". Zou and Feng (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.00526, opened): the Jacobian must be evaluated at a witness "which has satisfied all the constraints with degenerate in the GCS, for example, parallel and incident constraints", and the method's well- and under-constraint criteria "may give wrong results in some situations". MEC-O-SK-2 tests "known dependencies" against "exact rational rank", which passes only when test witnesses are exactly rational.
**Fix:** (1) Build witnesses constructively in the decomposition's order with rational choices (pick tangent points and directions, then centres along normals), so incidences hold exactly; use modular rank only on such exact witnesses. (2) Where no rational witness exists, compute rank at a Krawczyk-certified witness with interval arithmetic: a nonzero interval minor certifies full rank, and a deficiency is reported `Estimated` with the singular-value gap. (3) Certify under-determined blocks by applying Krawczyk to the square KKT system of the minimum-displacement problem, which is regular at a strict minimizer, so their points can be `Guaranteed`. (4) MEC-O-SK-2 adds witnesses with circles and tangencies whose coordinates are irrational.

### R9-02 · Critical · Targets: CONTRACT, ING, KLN
**Section:** CONTRACT 5 (`Truth`); ING "`Approx<T>` and the decision rule" (D-ING-7); KLN "Progressive results"
